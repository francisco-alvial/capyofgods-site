Description

A capybara climbs a colossal tower while its weapons fire on their own: bullet heaven crossed with classic platforming.

Capy of Gods is an action roguelike in which a capybara climbs a colossal tower while its weapons fire on their own. It mixes the wave combat of a bullet heaven with classic platforming and dodge-dashing, across procedurally generated floors, three climbs and more than 25 bosses. It is out now in Early Access on Steam, with a free demo.

Description

In Capy of Gods you do not shoot: you climb. The capybara's weapons attack on their own while the player focuses on climbing, dodging and choosing how to grow. The result is a roguelike that crosses two genres that rarely meet: the enemy tide of a bullet heaven in the style of Vampire Survivors, and classic vertical platforming with a dodge dash.

Every run starts at the bottom and climbs through procedurally generated levels, so no two ascents are the same. There are three climbs: the First 300 Floors (stone, moss and blood), the Labyrinths (snow, desert and volcano) and The Summit, the final chapter where every boss returns. Along the way there are shops, secret trap rooms, challenge statues, totems that curse the bosses, and more than 25 bosses with hand-made mechanics. You do not stumble into the floor boss: you summon it yourself, when you decide you are ready.

What you lose on death is not lost entirely. The rubies that leave the tower are persistent and are spent between runs on permanent attributes, weapons, instincts, items and new playable capybaras, so every failed attempt leaves something behind.

Capy of Gods is made by one person in Godot and ships translated into 29 languages.

Features

  • 12 playable capybaras, each with a signature starting weapon and a passive that grows with level
  • 12 automatic weapons, 26 animal instincts and 48 items that stack and combine
  • Three climbs and nine biomes, procedurally generated every run
  • More than 25 bosses with their own mechanics, and floor bosses you summon yourself
  • Persistent progression: rubies unlock attributes, weapons, instincts, items and characters through 150 missions
  • Global Steam leaderboards per map (most kills and fastest clear) and 98 achievements
  • 29 languages, full keyboard, mouse and controller support

History

Capy of Gods started in December 2025 as a small vertical-jumping prototype and grew into a full roguelike once automatic weapons and enemy waves were added on top of the platforming. Development is done by a single person under the CapyEmber name.

A free demo went live on Steam in July 2026 and was updated through the summer. The game took part in Steam Next Fest in October 2026 and launched in Early Access on November 9, 2026, with the full story from the first floor to the summit.

The story

The legend

There was a time when treasure hunters were the celebrities of the world: rich, famous, adored. Sir Capy Drake was the greatest of them all. Until one day he walked into one ancient ruin too many and dug up something that did not belong to this world.

Shortly after news of the find spread, Drake vanished without a trace. No body, no letter, not even a boot.

That same season, a colossal tower appeared out of nowhere in the snowy mountains north of the capital, Sweetwater. From its summit burns a red light that stains every dawn. That is why those mountains stopped being called the White Mountains and are known today as the Crimson Peaks.

Word spread that inside were thousands of treasures and objects of strange magic. Hunters and thieves from all over the world marched there, and the tavern at the foot of the tower became their refuge. On its walls hang the posters of every famous climber next to the wealth they have taken out of the tower: half hall of fame, half scoreboard.

Over the years the final rumour was born: at the very top waits a treasure capable of turning whoever holds it into a god.

And so began the maddest race the world has ever seen. They called it Capy of Gods.
