Weapons

Weapons fire on their own at the closest enemy and level up through level-up cards, improving damage, cadence, size or special properties. You carry up to three by default. Every character has a signature weapon, and once a weapon is unlocked anyone can find it.

Flamethrower

In the demoStarting weapon of Dr. Plague

Sprays a stream of flames that swivels toward the nearest enemy and may burn everything it sweeps across

Available from the start.

Razor Fur

In the demoStarting weapon of Johnny

A bristling fur aura that damages anything nearby

Available from the start.

Toxic Drool

In the demoStarting weapon of Bob

Spits venomous projectiles that bounce between enemies

Available from the start.

Claw Sweep

Full gameStarting weapon of Lycap

Rends enemies in a cone toward the nearest foe. On kill, 5% chance to heal 1 HP.

Mission: Death's Executioner. Defeat Death, the floor 3 boss, 3 times. Defeat Death (floor 3 boss) 3 times to unlock. 50 rubies

Fiery Spit

In the demoStarting weapon of C-Rex

Launches fireballs that burn monsters

Mission: Prehistoric Duel. Complete the Ghost Capybara's mission on floor 1 and defeat the C-Rex it summons. Complete the first floor's ghost mission to unlock. 50 rubies

Glow Stick

Full gameStarting weapon of Skelly-san

Throws a stick that slows down, turns around and flies back. It hits on the way out and back.

Mission: Ruined Concert. Return the torn banners to Skelly-san and defeat whatever comes through that door. Complete the floor 3 banner quest and defeat Skelly-san to unlock. 50 rubies

Octo Sentry

Full gameStarting weapon of Gary

Every 3 s deploys an octo sentry that lives 5 s and shoots nearby enemies using your stats.

Mission: Lisa's Executioner. Defeat Lisa, the ice mage of Labyrinths level 1, 3 times. Defeat Lisa (boss of the Labyrinths' snow level) 3 times to unlock. 50 rubies

Sniper Rifle

Full gameStarting weapon of Deadeye

Fires piercing shots that go through enemies in a line. Aim with the mouse or right stick

Mission: A Promise Is a Promise. Find Deadeye frozen on level 1 of the Labyrinths, hunt down the chanting wolves and defeat the yeti. Free Deadeye from the ice on level 1 of the Labyrinths and defeat the yeti to unlock. 50 rubies

Thunder Coil

In the demoStarting weapon of Sparky

Summons electric shocks on nearby enemies

Mission: Tower Executioner. Defeat 7500 enemies in total. 50 rubies

Toxic Stench

Full gameStarting weapon of Stinky

Leaves stink clouds in your wake as you move. They hit whoever is inside and may poison them; the poison stacks without limit.

Mission: Mushroom Harvest. Hunt 1000 Jelly Mushrooms in total (if they burst on their own, they don't count). Kill 1,000 Jelly Mushrooms to unlock. 50 rubies

Valiant Slash

Full gameStarting weapon of Sir Drake Jr.

Slashes an arc at the nearest foe. Every 3rd swing spins 360° with +100% crit chance.

Mission: A Hundred Thousand Down. Defeat 100,000 enemies in total. Defeat 100,000 enemies in total to unlock. 50 rubies

Wild Gust

In the demoStarting weapon of Capytain Gale

Blows wind gusts that chase enemies. Gusts fired in midair deal double damage.

Mission: Horde Tamer. Reach level 4 of the Raging Horde in a single run. 50 rubies
Nothing in the tower matches that.
