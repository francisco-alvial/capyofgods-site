Characters

Twelve capybaras hang on the tavern wall. Each one starts with a signature weapon and has a passive that grows as you level up: a small bonus every level and a bigger one every 10 levels. The number on each poster is the wealth that climber has taken out of the tower.

Base stats differ per character (health, speed, crit, armor). The reference climber is Bob: 150 health, 250 movement speed, 5% crit chance. Once the condition is met, the unlock is purchased with rubies in the Unlockables menu.

Per level: +1% crit chance per level · Every 10 levels: +10% luck every 10 levels
