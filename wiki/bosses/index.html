Bosses

Every boss in Capy of Gods is built around one idea. Semi-bosses appear on a timer during a floor and drop an elite chest. The floor boss is summoned by you at the Piano Skeleton. Phase bosses guard the exit of each phase. Bosses are immune to pushes, stuns and slows from items.

This page describes every boss, including the final encounters. Skip it if you want to be surprised.

Floor 1 · Stone

Shoggoth

Semi-boss

Flying horror that teleports around you and charges straight through. Read the tell before the dash.

Voidborn Goddess

Semi-boss

Predicts where you are going to dodge and drops her hand spell right there, followed by a spread of four projectiles.

Voidcaller

Floor boss

Summoned at the Piano Skeleton. Teleports around the arena and cycles dark flames, a laser and tentacles that rise from the ground. No timer: you decide when.

C-Rex

Mission boss

Appears if you accept the Ghost Capybara's mission on floor 1 and destroy the obelisks. Defeating it unlocks the Fiery Spit and starts the C-Rex chain.

Floor 2 · Moss

Forest Whale

Semi-boss

Floats over the moss, buffs the swarm around it and lobs a slow, heavy orb.

Cat Fight

Semi-boss

A ground brawler that walks the platforms and throws cats at you every half second, plus a giant cat after a charge-up.

Fat Plant

Floor boss

A huge carnivorous plant that roots itself on a platform near you and replants when you get far. Its spore rings poison anyone caught inside.

Floor 3 · Blood

Bringer of Death

Semi-boss

Teleports to a platform in view every few seconds, even when it is already close, then slashes and releases a fast burst of dark projectiles.

Bone Wheel

Semi-boss

A fast rolling wheel of bones that hurts on contact and never shoots. It spins up in place, marks a red line, and dashes along it.

Death

Floor boss

Throws a boomerang scythe and raises skeleton archers from the ground to swarm you. Defeat it three times to unlock the Claw Sweep.

Skelly-san

Mission boss

Comes through the pink door once you return the torn banners. Defeating it unlocks the Glow Stick and starts the Skelly-san chain.

End of Phase 1

The Devourer

Phase boss

Waits in a sealed room after floor 3. Steals the items from your backpack at the start and returns them as you hurt it. Charges, a mouth laser, a 360° sweeping laser and zigzag tackles across the whole room.

The Devourer's Mother

Phase boss

Arrives when the Devourer falls. Same kit with a double laser from both mouths, and it can birth Mini Devourers. Defeating her completes Phase 1 and unlocks the Labyrinths.
