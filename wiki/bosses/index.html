Bosses

Every boss in Capy of Gods is built around one idea. Semi-bosses arrive on the clock during a floor and drop an elite chest. Floor bosses are summoned by you at the Organ. Mission bosses wait behind side quests. Bosses are immune to pushes, stuns and slows, and their deaths are worth watching.

First 300 Floors · Floor 1 · Stone

Shoggoth

The Formless Horror
Semi-boss

Flying horror that teleports around you and charges straight through. Read the tell before the dash.

Voidborn Goddess

Daughter of the Void
Semi-boss

Predicts where you are going to dodge and drops her hand spell right there, followed by a spread of four projectiles.

Voidcaller

Herald of the Abyss
Floor boss

Teleports around the arena and cycles dark flames, a laser and tentacles that rise from the ground.

Play clip

C-Rex

Mission boss

Appears if you accept the Ghost Capybara's request and destroy the obelisks. Defeating it starts the C-Rex unlock chain.

First 300 Floors · Floor 2 · Moss

Forest Whale

Singer of the Deep Woods
Semi-boss

A flying whale that leaves a cloud of dust which makes nearby enemies stronger, fires a telegraphed dart and charges a slow energy ball.

Mantis

Reaper of the Moss
Semi-boss

A giant mantis that cycles three attacks: thrusts up close, a leaping slam marked on the ground and a cone of three blades.

Play clip

Fat Plant

The Swamp Glutton
Floor boss

A huge carnivorous plant that roots itself near you. Spore rings that poison, acid rain and a slow homing acid ball.

Play clip

First 300 Floors · Floor 3 · Blood

Bringer of Death

Messenger of the Last Breath
Semi-boss

Teleports to a platform in view every few seconds, slashes, fires a dark wave and drops portal hands right where you stand.

Bone Wheel

The Bone Mill
Semi-boss

A rolling wheel of bones. A burst of five ghost dashes, and a belly flop that falls from the sky on the mark under your feet.

Play clip

Death

The One No One Escapes
Floor boss

Throws its scythe like a boomerang from above and below, dives at you and raises skeleton archers.

Play clip

Skelly-san

Mission boss

Comes through the pink door once you return the torn banners. Defeating it starts the Skelly-san unlock chain.

The Labyrinths · Snow

Dino Rex

King of the Ice Age
Semi-boss

A giant dinosaur that freezes mid-air before a belly slam, crosses the room in a ghost dash and calls flags that rain balls from the sky.

Play clip

Pengu

Emperor of the Glacier
Semi-boss

A penguin in sunglasses: jumping pecks, ice blocks that fall in sequence and a sweeping beam.

Play clip

Lisa

Sorceress of the Frozen Tides
Floor boss

A flying sorceress with five spells: rising bubbles, a water explosion, heads from the ground, ice walls that close in and falling spikes.

Play clip

Yeti

Terror of the Peaks
Mission boss

Arrives when you try to free the frozen climber in the snow. Snowballs in an arc and a jump that raises a wave of ice.

The Labyrinths · Desert

Demon Samurai

The Masterless Blade
Semi-boss

A three-slash combo with a dash on each cut and a leaping strike. At half health it shouts, ignites its sword and gets faster and tougher.

Puppet H

The Stone Puppet
Semi-boss

A floating stone puppet whose closed eyes reveal the next spell: closing rock walls, pillars where you stand or crystal fists from above.

Play clip

Bertha

The Desert Axe
Floor boss

A giant goblin with an axe: heavy combos, stomps that send earth eruptions forward, leaping slams and a five-second tornado.

Play clip

The Labyrinths · Volcano

Morthag

Lord of Ash
Semi-boss

Plants itself and breathes a huge frontal flame, raises fire tornadoes around you and charges an explosion overhead.

Play clip

Demon Slime

Living Black Fire
Semi-boss

Starts as a bouncing slime with its own health bar. Then it transforms into a demon with a cleaver, triple jumps and black fire.

Megumi

The Salamander Witch
Floor boss

A salamander witch that sprints across the room casting black holes, fire tornadoes and fireballs. Every few spells her salamander becomes a dragon and fires a beam that crosses the whole level.

Play clip

First 300 Floors · The sealed room

Spoilers hidden. This section talks about the last encounters of the game.

The Devourer

Final boss

Waits in a sealed room after floor 3, shielded by blood hearts you must stand on. Steals your items and returns them as you hurt it. Charges, mouth lasers and zigzag tackles.

Play clip

The Devourer's Mother

Final boss

Arrives when the Devourer falls. More hearts, double lasers, and she can birth smaller devourers. Defeating her completes the First 300 Floors.

Play clip

The Labyrinths · The last vigil

Spoilers hidden. This section talks about the last encounters of the game.

Morrigal, The Last Vigil

Final boss

A dark knight with two health bars who steals your weapons and gives them back as you fight. Every attack has a physical version and, after the transformation, an explosive one. Vigil flames keep it shielded.

Play clip

The Summit

Spoilers hidden. This section talks about the last encounters of the game.

CapyDemon King

Final boss

The one who summons every boss of The Summit, one every two minutes. Fights from the organ at the end of each level with cages of light, pulses and lasers, then retreats through a portal. Waits on the throne at the top.

Nothing in the tower matches that.
