Three climbs

Before each run you choose a climb. Each one has its own map, its own difficulties and its own unlock chain.

  • First 300 Floors: three floors of 100, one per biome (stone, moss, blood), then the sealed room of the Devourer. The free demo covers the first floor of this climb.
  • The Labyrinths: unlocked by completing the First 300 Floors. The tower breaks into wide levels of interconnected rooms with side doors and breaches, across snow, desert and volcano, and ends at the last vigil.
  • The Summit: unlocked by completing the Labyrinths. Two levels of 20 minutes where the tower changes biome as you climb and a boss is summoned every two minutes, then the throne room. The story ends here.

Every floor is generated from scratch each run: platforms, traps, chests, secret rooms, shrines and decoration follow the biome's rules.

Difficulty

Each climb has four difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard and Capy of Gods. Each one is unlocked by beating the previous one. Higher difficulties speed up the difficulty clock (×1, ×1.5, ×2 and ×3), so enemies grow stronger faster, and bosses get much more health. In exchange you take home more rubies and experience.

Inside a floor the pressure also ramps with time: enemy health grows with every minute and their damage carries over to the next floor.

Events on the clock

  1. 0:00 Waves start. Every 30 seconds an elite spawns.
  2. Every few minutes The Goblin Thief pops out of the ground in view.
  3. Minute 3 Ranged enemies join the waves.
  4. On the bar Semi-bosses and events arrive at the times marked on the progress bar, with a warning first.
  5. Minute 5 (moss) The carnivorous plant tide.
  6. Minute 10 The bar breaks and the Horde of Lost Souls begins.

Missions inside the tower

  • The Ghost Capybara (floor 1): accept its request, destroy the flying obelisks and face the C-Rex.
  • Skelly-san's banners (floor 3): the pink door asks you to recover three torn banners from the skeletal hounds. Return them and defeat what comes through the door.
  • The frozen climber (snow labyrinth): someone is trapped in a block of ice. Help them and something big hears the noise.
  • The Architect: clear the first 100 floors enough times and something in the main menu changes.

Leaderboards and achievements

Every map has two global Steam leaderboards: most kills in a single run and fastest clear. Both are visible from the main menu with your own position. The game also has 98 Steam achievements, tied to the missions of the tavern board.
