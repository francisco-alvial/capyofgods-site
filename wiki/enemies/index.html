Enemies

Each biome has its own cast. Enemies grow tougher with time on the floor, and every 30 seconds an elite version of a regular enemy spawns: bigger, far tougher, hits harder and drops much more experience. Ranged enemies only join from minute 3 of each floor. Wave enemies never spawn inside your view.

Stone (floor 1)

Eyeball

Flying chaser. Passes through walls, hurts on contact.

Worm

Ground ranged enemy. Stops at a distance and shoots.

Mosquito

Flying dasher. Idles, then lunges past you.

Time Bomb

Flying kamikaze. Counts 3, 2, 1 and explodes in an area.

Mage Cultist

Walks the platforms and casts a projectile every few seconds.

Assassin Cultist

Fast melee cultist.

Big Cultist

Slow, heavy melee cultist.

Moss (floor 2)

Mushroom

Melee chaser that throws itself at you like a projectile.

Frogger

Hops around and hits you with its tongue from a distance.

Bug

Flying chaser. Hurts on contact.

Giant Fly

Flying dasher.

Bee

Approaches flying, then fires itself as a straight, accelerating dart.

Jelly Mushroom

Poison kamikaze. Grows, turns green and bursts into poison.

Fire Pepper

Ranged caster in four colours.

Evil Vine

The minute 5 event: carnivorous vines rise across the tower.

Blood (floor 3)

Bones Gladiator

Melee with a thrust and a short dash. Attacks from further than it looks.

Dark Knight

Heavy melee: two slashes, each with a long dash.

Infected Exploder

Tank kamikaze that runs at your face and bursts, even if you kill it first.

Beholder

Flying chaser that bites with a short lunge.

Blood Dragon

Charges the spot where you were when it started, and claws when close.

BatZ

Ranged flyer that never chases. Comes in squads of five.

Sentinel Eye

Patrols a fixed height. If it sees you and survives five seconds, it triggers the Eye Swarm: red lanes full of eyes crossing the tower.

Snow (Labyrinths)

Ice Golem

Heavy melee with two different attacks.

Phantom Knight

Agile melee with a crescent slash.

Water Wisp

Flying chaser.

Reaper

Fast flying chaser.

Imp

Ranged flyer that keeps its distance and fires bolts.

Winter Wolf

Sleeps under a spell until you get close. Only appears in a side quest.

Desert (Labyrinths)

Djinn Bandit

Fast melee with a sickle slash.

Red Ogre

Heavy katana slash.

Wasp

Flying chaser.

Oculothorax

Bites and flees, bites and flees.

Earth Wisp

Ranged flyer that fires stone bolts.

Wind Wisp

Glows red and releases a shockwave. Does not die doing it.

Volcano (Labyrinths)

Executioner Demon

Drives its greatsword down and follows with a long thrust.

Curse Demon

Plants itself and fires a cursed beam in front of it.

Tharnok Demon

Leaps to where you stood and splashes fire.

Burning Skull

Flying chaser.

Alkara Demon

Flying caster that alternates fireballs and dives.

Demon Mine

Floating mine that glows redder and redder until it explodes.

Large Skull

One per minute. Hovers above you and breathes fire down.

Everywhere

Lost Soul

The only enemy of the Horde (after minute 10), tinted with the colour of the current level.

Goblin Thief

Pops out of the ground every few minutes and runs. Every hit drops gold or rubies; killing it makes it burst with the rest.

Wendigo

Challenge enemy of the Challenge Statues on stone and snow. Falls from a beam of light.

Fish-man

Challenge enemy on moss and desert. Claws and throws water balls in an arc.

ExperimentZ

Challenge enemy on blood and volcano. Tackles you from far away.

Nothing in the tower matches that.
