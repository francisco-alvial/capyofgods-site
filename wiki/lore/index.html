Lore

The legend

There was a time when treasure hunters were the celebrities of the world: rich, famous, adored. Sir Capy Drake was the greatest of them all. Until one day he walked into one ancient ruin too many and dug up something that did not belong to this world.

Shortly after news of the find spread, Drake vanished without a trace. No body, no letter, not even a boot.

That same season, a colossal tower appeared out of nowhere in the snowy mountains north of the capital, Sweetwater. From its summit burns a red light that stains every dawn. That is why those mountains stopped being called the White Mountains and are known today as the Crimson Peaks.

Word spread that inside were thousands of treasures and objects of strange magic. Hunters and thieves from all over the world marched there, and the tavern at the foot of the tower became their refuge. On its walls hang the posters of every famous climber next to the wealth they have taken out of the tower: half hall of fame, half scoreboard.

Over the years the final rumour was born: at the very top waits a treasure capable of turning whoever holds it into a god.

And so began the maddest race the world has ever seen. They called it Capy of Gods.

The climbers

The number on each poster in the tavern is not a bounty on that capybara's head: it is the wealth that climber has already pulled out of the tower. The bigger the number, the more famous the climber.

The rest of the story is told inside the tower. It is not written here on purpose.
