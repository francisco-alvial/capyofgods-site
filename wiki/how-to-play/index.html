Aim the Sniper RifleMouseRight stick

Your weapons fire on their own and pick the closest enemy. You never aim, with one exception: the Sniper Rifle can be aimed by hand with the mouse or the right stick. Your job is to move, jump, dash and choose. PlayStation controllers show their own button icons.

The dash

The dash is your dodge. While dashing you are invincible, so it is the answer to boss charges, lasers and projectile walls. It also gives you horizontal reach for platforming. It has a short cooldown, so do not waste it.

Experience and level-ups

Enemies drop watermelons: that is your experience. Collect them to level up. Each level-up deals three cards face down: a new weapon, an upgrade to a weapon you carry, or an animal instinct (a passive). Epic and legendary cards flip last, with a bit of suspense. You can reroll the hand or discard a card you never want to see again in this run.

Higher rarities give bigger upgrades and your luck shifts the odds toward them. You carry up to three weapons and three instincts by default. The Weapon Slot and Instinct Slot attributes, bought with rubies, add up to two more of each.

Gold, chests and the shop

Enemies also drop gold, which only lasts for the current run. Spend it on chests scattered around the tower (each one costs a bit more than the last), at the merchant's shop, or at the fusion machine, which merges items into one of higher rarity. Chests give items: passive objects that stack in your backpack. The Fake Coin item gives a chance to open chests for free.

Structures worth knowing

  • Dark Pact: hit it with your weapons enough times and it offers power in exchange for maximum health.
  • Mouth of God: trades gold for health, in five tiers.
  • Demon Totem: curses the bosses. From then on every semi-boss and floor boss on that floor comes with an extra copy and more health, and every copy drops its own chest.
  • Challenge Statue: wakes up three tough challenge enemies. Defeat them all and a golden chest falls where the last one died.
  • Capybara Hot Springs: the first bath in each spring is free and heals half your health. The next ones ask for a tip.
  • Secret rooms: hidden side rooms full of traps (cannons, lasers, fire bars, crushers, electric arcs and more) with a golden chest at the end.
  • Breakable crates: smash them for rubies.

Summoning the floor boss

Near the top of each floor there is an Organ played by a skeleton. Interact with it to summon the floor boss. There is no timer forcing you: keep farming to arrive stronger, or call it early to save time on the leaderboard. Defeating the floor boss closes the floor: you get a short grace period, a portal opens to the next floor, and if you stay the horde arrives. You keep your weapons, instincts, items, level and gold. The tower regenerates.

The Horde of Lost Souls

After 10 minutes on a floor the progress bar breaks and the Horde of Lost Souls begins: the tower fills with souls that get faster and tougher with every level of the bar. Their colour tells you the current level. The horde does not pay gold or rubies, so staying too long rarely pays off. Reaching high horde levels is still a mission goal for the brave.

Danger warnings

Nothing big appears out of nowhere. Bosses are announced with hazard tapes and a siren a few seconds before they arrive, and events (ambushes, the plant tide, the eye swarm) flash their own warning first. Use those seconds to find a good platform.

Dying

Death ends the run, but not the progress. You keep every ruby you collected and every mission counter you advanced. The recap screen shows your damage by source, the run summary and anything you unlocked.
