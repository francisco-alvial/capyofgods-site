Progression & unlocks

Rubies are the persistent currency. Elites and bosses drop them for sure, regular enemies with a small chance, and the Goblin Thief drips them on every hit. Between runs, spend them in the Unlockables menu on permanent attributes, weapons, instincts, items and characters. Most unlockables first require a mission.

Unlock chains

  • C-Rex: complete the Ghost Capybara's mission on floor 1 and defeat the C-Rex → buy the Fiery Spit → C-Rex becomes purchasable.
  • Skelly-san: complete the banner quest on floor 3 and defeat Skelly-san → buy the Glow Stick → Skelly-san becomes purchasable.
  • Strange Potion: leave 20 tips at the Capybara Springs.
  • Valiant Slash: complete Phase 2 (Labyrinths) → then 10,000 kills with it unlock Sir Drake Jr.
