Progression & unlocks

Rubies are the persistent currency. Elites and bosses drop them for sure, regular enemies with a small chance, crates break into them and the Goblin Thief drips them on every hit. Between runs, spend them in the Shop of the main menu on permanent attributes, weapons, instincts, items and characters. Most unlockables first require a mission from the tavern board.

Permanent attributes

Each attribute has levels and the cost grows with every level. Some attributes require a mission before they can be bought. The Toggler lets you switch off content you bought so it stops showing up in your runs.

Unlock chains

  • C-Rex: complete the Ghost Capybara's mission on floor 1 and defeat the C-Rex, buy the Fiery Spit, and C-Rex becomes purchasable.
  • Skelly-san: complete the banner quest on floor 3 and defeat Skelly-san, buy the Glow Stick, and Skelly-san becomes purchasable.
  • Sniper Rifle: free the frozen climber in the snow labyrinth.
  • Strange Potion: leave 20 tips at the Capybara Hot Springs.
  • Climbs: completing the First 300 Floors opens the Labyrinths, and completing the Labyrinths opens The Summit.
