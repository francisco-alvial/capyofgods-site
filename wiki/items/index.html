Items

Items come from chests, the shop, the vending machine and boss drops. They go into your backpack and stack. Rarity determines both how strong the effect is and how rare the drop is. Luck shifts the odds toward higher rarities.

Once the condition is met, the unlock is purchased with rubies in the Unlockables menu. The demo contains a curated subset of the content. Anything marked “Full game” is only in the complete version.
