Is Capy of Gods out?
Yes. It launched in Early Access on Steam on November 9, 2026. The free demo is still available.
Why Early Access if the story is complete?
The story is complete from the first floor to the summit, with its ending and credits. Early Access is for balancing it with players and adding more: new capybaras, rewards for The Summit and an endless mode. The plan is to stay in Early Access for roughly three to six months.
How much does it cost?
USD 7.99, with regional prices on Steam. Updates are free and the price will not go up at 1.0.
Which platforms?
PC through Steam: Windows, Linux and macOS.
What is in the demo?
The first floor of the First 300 Floors, a curated set of characters, weapons, instincts and items, and the demo leaderboards. Progress in the demo does not carry over, but demo players get a ruby gift in the full game.
Does it support controllers?
Yes, fully. Xbox and PlayStation layouts with their own button icons, and every menu works with keyboard, mouse and gamepad. The interface was sized with the Steam Deck in mind.
Which languages?
29: English, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), French, Italian, German, Danish, Finnish, Indonesian, Malay, Norwegian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Swedish, Czech, Polish, Hungarian, Turkish, Romanian, Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Greek, Vietnamese, Dutch, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
Does it have cloud saves and achievements?
Yes. Progress syncs with Steam Cloud and there are 98 Steam achievements.
Can I stream or make videos of it?
Yes. Record, stream and monetize freely. No permission needed and no content-ID claims.
Who makes it?
CapyEmber, a one-person studio from Chile. The game is built with the Godot engine.
Is there co-op or multiplayer?
No. Capy of Gods is a single-player game with global leaderboards.
Where do I report bugs or suggest things?
On the community Discord or in the Steam discussions. Every report is read.

This wiki is maintained by the developer and reflects the current Early Access build. Numbers may change with updates. Last updated: 2026-11-12.
