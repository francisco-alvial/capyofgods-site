FAQ

When does Capy of Gods release?

The full game is planned for October 2026, right after Steam Next Fest. The free demo is available now on Steam.

How much will it cost?

The price of the full game has not been announced yet. The demo is free.

Which platforms?

PC through Steam: Windows, Linux and macOS. The demo already ships for all three.

What is in the demo?

The first 100 floors of Phase 1 (the stone biome), a curated set of characters, weapons, instincts and items, the two Steam leaderboards and the Goblin Thief. Everything marked “Full game” in this wiki is not in the demo.

Does it support controllers?

Yes, fully. Xbox layout by default, and every menu works with both keyboard and gamepad. Steam Deck sizes were taken into account for the UI.

Which languages?

19: English, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), French, Italian, German, Danish, Finnish, Indonesian, Malay, Norwegian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Swedish, Czech, Polish, Hungarian, Turkish and Romanian.

Can I stream or make videos of it?

Yes. Record, stream and monetize freely. No permission needed and no content-ID claims.

Who makes it?

CapyEmber, a one-person studio from Chile. The game is built with the Godot engine.

Is there co-op or multiplayer?

No. Capy of Gods is a single-player game with global leaderboards.

Where do I report bugs or suggest things?

On the community Discord, or through the Steam community hub of the demo.

How do I support the game?

Wishlist it on Steam. It is the single most useful thing you can do before launch.

The wiki is maintained by the developer and reflects the current build. Numbers may change with updates. Last updated: 2026-09-07.
