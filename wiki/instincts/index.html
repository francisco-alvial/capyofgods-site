Instincts

Instincts are the passives of Capy of Gods. They appear as level-up cards alongside weapons and stack up to a maximum level. Most of them are unlocked with missions and then purchased with rubies.

Once the condition is met, the unlock is purchased with rubies in the Unlockables menu. The demo contains a curated subset of the content. Anything marked “Full game” is only in the complete version.

Stone Skin

In the demo

Your skin hardens like rock: gain armor that reduces damage taken

Mission: Weathered Hide. Take 2000 damage in total. 100 rubies

Wild Reflexes

In the demo

Increases attack speed

Available from the start.

Balanced Instinct

Full game

Each level raises damage, max health, movement speed and attack speed at once

Mission: A Hundred Crowns. Defeat 100 floor bosses in total. 100 rubies

Chaotic Instinct

Full game

Each level boosts a random stat, stronger than its own instinct would

Mission: Loot King. Open 200 chests in total. 100 rubies

Keen Eye

Full game

Increases the chance to land critical hits

Mission: Weak Points. Land 5000 critical hits in total. 100 rubies

Ferocious Bite

Full game

Increases critical hit damage

Mission: Elite Hunter. Defeat 50 elite enemies in total. 100 rubies

Sharp Fangs

In the demo

Increases damage of all attacks

Available from the start.

Dark Mark

In the demo

More enemies with more health and a bit more damage, but they drop more gold

Mission: Dark Signatures. Complete 12 Dark Pacts in total. 100 rubies

Slippery Fur

Full game

Increases the chance to dodge enemy attacks

Mission: Not a Scratch. Dodge 150 attacks in total. 100 rubies

Long Fuse

Full game

Extends the duration of the effects you cause

Mission: Slow Burn. Defeat 500 enemies with burn damage in total. 100 rubies

Ancestral Instinct

In the demo

Gain more experience per enemy

Mission: Veteran Blood. Reach level 25 in a single run. 100 rubies

Greedy Snout

Full game

Increases the gold dropped by enemies

Mission: Full Pockets. Collect 50000 gold in total. 100 rubies

Young Blood

Full game

All healing you receive is more effective

Mission: Second Wind. Recover 5000 health in total. 100 rubies

Bloodthirst

Full game

Increases the life steal of your direct hits

Mission: Bloodbath. Defeat 12500 enemies in total. 100 rubies

Fortune Whiskers

In the demo

Increases the chance of higher rarity rewards

Mission: Treasure Seeker. Open 15 chests in total. 100 rubies

Keen Nose

Full game

Attracts experience orbs from farther away

Mission: Eternal Learner. Collect 100000 experience in total. 100 rubies

Thick Hide

In the demo

Increases maximum health

Available from the start.

Swift Paws

In the demo

Increases movement speed

Available from the start.

Wild Momentum

Full game

Your projectiles fly faster and travel farther

Mission: Lethal Drool. Defeat 3000 enemies with Toxic Drool. 100 rubies

Extra Projectile

Full game

Your weapons fire more projectiles. Does not affect Razor Fur, Flamethrower or Toxic Stench.

Mission: Legendary Armorer. Get any weapon to level 40 in a single run. 100 rubies

Steady Heartbeat

Full game

Recover a percentage of your max health every second

Mission: Oak Heart. Recover 15000 health in total. 100 rubies

Shell

Full game

Gain a blue shield that absorbs damage and regenerates when you avoid hits

Mission: Steel Ribs. Take 10000 damage in total. 100 rubies

Coated Claws

Full game

Increases the chance to apply status effects; above 100% it applies extra stacks

Mission: Elite Executioner. Defeat 200 elite enemies in total. 100 rubies

Scavenger's Instinct

Full game

Increases damage to enemies with an active status effect

Mission: Ash Rain. Defeat 1500 enemies with burn damage in total. 100 rubies

Spiked Fur

Full game

Enemies that touch you lose part of their max health. Does not affect bosses.

Mission: Anvil Skin. Take 20000 damage in total. 100 rubies

Overgrowth

Full game

Makes your weapons and projectiles bigger

Mission: Demolisher. Destroy 500 breakable objects in total. 100 rubies
