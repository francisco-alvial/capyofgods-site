Instincts

Instincts are the passives of Capy of Gods. They appear as level-up cards next to weapons and stack up to a maximum level. Most of them are unlocked through missions and then bought with rubies.

Once the condition is met, the unlock is bought with rubies in the Shop of the main menu. The free demo contains the first floor and a curated part of the content. Anything marked “Full game” is only in the complete version.

Sharp Fangs

In the demo

Increases damage of all attacks

Available from the start.

Swift Paws

In the demo

Increases movement speed

Available from the start.

Thick Hide

In the demo

Increases maximum health

Available from the start.

Wild Reflexes

In the demo

Increases attack speed

Available from the start.

Ancestral Instinct

In the demo

Gain more experience per enemy

Mission: Veteran Blood. Reach level 25 in a single run. 40 rubies

Balanced Instinct

Full game

Each level raises damage, max health, movement speed and attack speed at once

Mission: Summit Balance. Clear The Summit by defeating its final boss. 40 rubies

Bloodthirst

Full game

You steal life from your enemies: every direct hit heals you. Every 100% life steal = 1 health per hit.

Mission: Bloodbath. Defeat 12500 enemies in total. 40 rubies

Chaotic Instinct

Full game

Each level boosts a random stat. Higher rarity hits harder: at legendary it's worth double its own instinct

Mission: Loot King. Open 200 chests in total. 40 rubies

Coated Claws

Full game

Increases the chance to apply status effects; above 100% it applies extra stacks

Mission: Devourer's Claws. Clear the First 300 Floors by defeating the Devourer and its Mother. 40 rubies

Dark Mark

In the demo

More enemies with more health and a bit more damage, but they drop more gold

Mission: Dark Signatures. Complete 80 Dark Pacts in total. 40 rubies

Extra Projectile

Full game

Your weapons fire more projectiles. Does not affect Razor Fur, Flamethrower or Toxic Stench.

Mission: Legendary Armorer. Get any weapon to level 50 in a single run. 40 rubies

Ferocious Bite

Full game

Increases critical hit damage

Mission: Fangs of the Vigil. Defeat Morrigal, the final boss of the Labyrinths. 40 rubies

Fortune Whiskers

In the demo

Increases the chance of higher rarity rewards

Mission: Treasure Seeker. Open 15 chests in total. 40 rubies

Greedy Snout

Full game

Increases the gold dropped by enemies

Mission: Full Pockets. Collect 50000 gold in total. 40 rubies

Keen Eye

Full game

Increases the chance to land critical hits

Mission: Death's Gaze. Defeat Death, the floor 3 boss. 40 rubies

Keen Nose

Full game

Attracts experience orbs from farther away

Mission: Eternal Learner. Collect 100000 experience in total. 40 rubies

Long Fuse

Full game

Extends the duration of the effects you cause

Mission: Slow Burn. Defeat 500 enemies with burn damage in total. 40 rubies

Overgrowth

Full game

Makes your weapons and projectiles bigger

Mission: Demolisher. Destroy 1000 breakable objects in total. 40 rubies

Scavenger's Instinct

Full game

Increases damage to enemies with an active status effect

Mission: Ash Rain. Defeat 5000 enemies with burn damage in total. 40 rubies

Shell

Full game

Gain a blue shield that absorbs damage and regenerates when you avoid hits

Mission: Steel Ribs. Take 10000 damage in total. 40 rubies

Slippery Fur

Full game

Increases the chance to dodge enemy attacks

Mission: Not a Scratch. Dodge 150 attacks in total. 40 rubies

Spiked Fur

Full game

Your fur bristles with spikes: enemies that touch you lose part of their max health. Bosses, with tougher hides, only lose a tenth as much.

Mission: Anvil Skin. Take 20000 damage in total. 40 rubies

Steady Heartbeat

Full game

Recover a percentage of your max health every second

Mission: Oak Heart. Recover 15000 health in total. 40 rubies

Stone Skin

In the demo

Your skin hardens like rock: gain armor that reduces damage taken

Mission: Weathered Hide. Take 2000 damage in total. 40 rubies

Wild Momentum

Full game

Your projectiles fly faster and travel farther

Mission: Lethal Drool. Defeat 3000 enemies with Toxic Drool. 40 rubies

Young Blood

Full game

All healing you receive is more effective

Mission: Second Wind. Recover 5000 health in total. 40 rubies
Nothing in the tower matches that.
