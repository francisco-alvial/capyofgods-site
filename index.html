Climb the tower. Survive the horde. Become a god.

An action roguelike about a capybara climbing a colossal tower while waves of enemies try to drag it back down. Vertical platforming meets automatic, bullet-heaven combat. Every run builds a brand-new tower.

Play the free demoWatch the trailer

Free demo out now on Steam · Full game planned for October 2026 · Windows, Linux and macOS

Six ways to fall in

✦

Fight as you climb

Weapons fire automatically. You focus on climbing, jumping and dashing to dodge, then choose from three upgrades that rewrite your build every level.

✦

A tower that builds itself

Platforms, traps, chests, secret rooms and biomes (stone, moss, blood, and beyond) are generated fresh every single run.

✦

Bosses with real mechanics

Flying semi-bosses with unique attack patterns, and a floor boss you summon yourself, when you decide you are ready.

✦

Persistent progression

Earn rubies every run to unlock permanent attributes, weapons, animal instincts, items and new playable capybaras.

✦

Platforming with teeth

A precise dodge dash and true verticality running underneath the bullet-heaven chaos.

✦

Race the world

Two global Steam leaderboards run live during the demo: most kills in a single run, and fastest floor-1 clear.
