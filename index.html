Capy of Gods

How the climb feels

01

Fight as you climb

Weapons fire on their own. You focus on climbing, jumping and dashing through the swarm, then pick one of three cards that rewrites your build every level.

02

Bosses with real mechanics

More than 25 bosses, each built around one idea: a witch that turns into a dragon, a goblin that spins into a tornado, a death that throws its scythe like a boomerang. You summon the floor boss yourself, when you decide you are ready.

03

A tower that builds itself

Platforms, traps, chests, secret trap rooms, shrines and statues are generated fresh every run, across nine biomes that go from mossy stone to frozen labyrinths and a volcano.

04

Builds that get out of hand

Stack weapons, instincts and items until the screen becomes a storm. Chests roll rarities with your luck, the fusion machine merges items and dark pacts trade health for power.

Three climbs, one summit

Every run starts at the tavern. Pick a capybara, pick your climb and pick a difficulty.

01

First 300 Floors

Stone · Moss · Blood

Three floors of 100, one biome each, and a sealed room at the end where something enormous is waiting. The free demo covers the first floor.

02

The Labyrinths

Snow · Desert · Volcano

The tower breaks into wide levels of connected rooms. Blizzards, sandstorms and lava, a new cast of enemies and bosses, and a last vigil standing at the exit.

03

The Summit

Every biome, every boss

The final climb. Two long levels where the tower changes biome as you go up and a boss arrives every two minutes. Whoever summons them waits at the very top.

Meet the bosses

Semi-bosses on the clock, floor bosses you summon, mission bosses behind side quests and a few final encounters that are better seen than read about.
